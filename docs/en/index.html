03Guest accounts and cloud saves

The game supports guest authentication and optional linking to a persistent account. Recovery may be limited if an unlinked guest loses its authentication credentials.

The version under development has been verified in a staging environment for guest authentication, protected cloud-profile saving and restoration, one-time server credits and pre-authorized profile migration. Profiles may contain a player name, progress, equipment, in-game balances and settings. Existing local saves are not uploaded automatically. This does not mean that an earlier installed game version already uses these features.

The final notice must reflect the released cloud-save behaviour. When cloud saves become active, the profile data, purposes and retention must be documented accordingly.

07Access and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability; objection and withdrawal of consent may also apply. You can complain to the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

Send requests to the contact address above once it has been finalised. We may request information needed to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card information.

08Delete game data or a Unity account?

The game profile and Unity Player Account are different records and services. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete cloud data. The game controller handles deletion requests for game-related data.

Manage the separate Unity Player Account on Unity’s own website. Deleting it does not by itself delete all data held by other integrated games; a separate game-data request may be needed.

09Audience, advertising and cookies

ArcherFW is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. Concerns involving a child’s data can be raised with the finalised privacy contact.

This notice page has no own analytics, cookie-setting code, embedded trackers, forms or external fonts. External linked pages have their own policies.

Advertising, analytics and purchasing SDKs in the shipped game require a separate review. A tracker-free notice page does not mean that the game’s cloud services process no data.
